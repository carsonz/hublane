# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc].

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories][advisories] or by contacting the maintainers listed
on the repository profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be
respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate. Consequences
follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines][cc-enforcement].

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[cc-enforcement]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines
[advisories]: https://github.com/carsonz/hublane/security/advisories
